P — Problem Statement & Background

Organizations, restaurants, and individuals find it difficult to track surplus food resources efficiently, leading to massive avoidable food waste. Existing manual logs lack real-time donor-recipient matching, geolocation awareness, and automated notifications.

Smart Cloth Management System

P — Problem Statement & Background

Managing fabric storage manually is time-consuming and risks textile damage due to undetected temperature, humidity, and gas buildup in storage environments.

T — Technical Architecture & Stack

Built around ESP32 Microcontroller hardware, Arduino IDE framework, Embedded C++, DHT11 temperature/humidity sensors, gas sensors, relay modules, and a responsive web monitoring dashboard.

R — My Key Engineering Role & Implementation

I wired the hardware sensor components, programmed the microcontroller firmware in Embedded C++, calibrated feedback threshold triggers, and established WiFi telemetry data transmission to the web dashboard.

L — Key Technical Learnings & Skills Acquired

Gained hands-on experience in microcontroller hardware interfacing, sensor sampling logic, relay control loops, embedded C++, and IoT-to-web telemetry data flow.
